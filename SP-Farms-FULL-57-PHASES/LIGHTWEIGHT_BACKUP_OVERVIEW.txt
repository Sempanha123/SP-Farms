SP-Farms lightweight backup design

YES, the new phase pack supports:
- one account -> one available device
- multiple selected accounts -> multiple available devices
- queued accounts waiting for a free device
- automatic next-account assignment after release
- bound-device-first, any-available, LRU, round-robin, preferred-provider policies
- batch backup and batch restore
- small per-account workspace snapshots

Small backup means SP-Farms metadata/preferences/references, not a full emulator image.

Excluded from normal snapshots:
- Facebook private app data
- cookies
- plaintext passwords
- raw access tokens
- 2FA recovery secrets
- large media files

This keeps snapshots typically in KBs to low MBs depending on optional metadata/thumbnails.
